## Acquisition plan

Six moves. The gym stays on Glofox until the transfer. Do this from the desk and from Glofox. Do not email all 2,445 people.

1. **Class packages first.** 329 people already paid for classes. When the pack has one class left, the coach or the desk offers the $150 month before they go back to single classes. This is the warmest paid list.

2. **Free intro, the same week.** 367 people took a free class and did not become the 144. The follow-up is one call or one text in the week of the intro: come back for a four-week start, then the $150 month. Count a win only when the dues plan is in date.

3. **PAYG only if they came recently.** 1,537 PAYG names are mostly old. Before anyone is contacted, filter the Glofox file to Last Booking inside the last 90 days. Call that short list. Leave the rest alone.

4. **Eleven expired standard members.** One $150 and ten $120 were already expired on 11 November. One personal call each. No group blast.

5. **Parents on the floor.** 18 youth memberships are paid ($85 or $75) and 6 youth are complimentary. Ask the adult who brings the child. A parent who trains is a second paying adult, and that person counts toward the 300.

6. **Scoreboard at the desk.** Each Monday, write four numbers: paying adults in date, net new this week, cancelled this week, still to go until 300. The 26-a-month pace is about 6 net new a week.

Stop rule: if a person has not booked in 90 days, they are not in this round. The list is for the people already in the gym.
