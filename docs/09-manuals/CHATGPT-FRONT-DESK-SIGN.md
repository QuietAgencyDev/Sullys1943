## The sign

Portrait. Cream field. A Sully-red bar, about 14 points, down the entire left edge. A thin brown line inset from the other three edges, like a mat, so the frame has something to hold.

Left half, or slightly more: the avatar, full body, standing on the cream. He faces the reader and offers his open hand toward the words. He is welcoming people to the desk, not throwing a punch.

Right half, the words, stacked, easy to read from a step away:

**SIGN IN.**

**BOOK A CLASS.**

**THEN STEP ON THE FLOOR.**

Under those three lines, one smaller line in brown:

A booking is not a check-in. Scan in at the desk.

Nothing else. Do not add “download the app,” prices, class names, or coach names.

---

## What good looks like

Someone standing at the desk can read the three lines without leaning in. The avatar is the host. The red bar and the brown rule are the only decoration. The cream stays the paper colour, not a photograph of a gym behind him.
