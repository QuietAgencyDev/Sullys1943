### The Monday scoreboard

Write these four numbers every Monday, for the week that just ended. Monday to Sunday.

| Word | What to count | What not to count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Adults in date** | Standard adult monthly dues whose end date is still today or later. Ages 19+. On 11 November 2026 this was **144**: 141 people at $150 and 3 people at $120. | PAYG, free intro, class packs, flex passes, promo plans, personal training, youth, complimentary youth, expired dues, and the raw 2,446 names. |
| **Net new this week** | People who entered Adults in date during the week, minus Cancellations. This is the number that moves the stock. About **6 a week** keeps the 26-a-month pace. | A tour, a free class, a class pack, a promise, or a name already in the 144. |
| **Cancellations** | People who were Adults in date at the start of the week and are not Adults in date at the end. Expired, cancelled, or frozen. | A missed class by someone whose dues are still in date. A youth leaving. A PAYG who stops coming. |
| **Remaining to 300** | 300 minus Adults in date, after this week’s count. On 11 November 2026 this was **156**. | A guess. Recalculate it from the new Adults in date. Do not leave it at 156. |

The four numbers have to agree:

Adults in date this Monday = Adults in date last Monday + Net new this week.

Net new this week = new standard adult dues this week − Cancellations.

Remaining to 300 = 300 − Adults in date this Monday.

Gross joins this week = Net new this week + Cancellations. The month needs 26 net, so the gross number is higher whenever someone cancels.
