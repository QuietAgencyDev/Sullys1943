Platform Cost Summary

What Sully’s pays · what Quiet Agency donates · Helcim & QuickBooks separate

Prepared by: Quiet Agency For: Sully’s Boxing Gym (non-profit) Date: August 2026 Currency: Canadian dollars (approx.)
Partnership: Quiet Agency is donating the design, build, and ongoing product/engineering maintenance of this platform. Sully’s is a non-profit community gym and the flagship test site for Quiet Agency’s Boxing Digital Performance Platform. You are not charged a Quiet Agency maintenance or software license fee.

1. What Sully’s pays each month

Hosting only — under CAD $100 / month

This covers the live website, member app, staff tools, API, and database (Cloudflare, Vercel, Railway, Neon, domain). Quiet Agency operates the stack; Sully’s reimburses actual hosting at cost, capped under $100 CAD / mo.

2. What Quiet Agency donates (no fee to Sully’s)

  • Custom platform build (website, member portal, desk, coach timer / TV, owner desk, waivers, family, kitchen path, docs)
  • Maintenance / Care retain — waived (bugs, deploys, small improvements, guidance)
  • Engineering for Helcim + QuickBooks connections when you are ready (you still pay those vendors)
  • Ongoing platform R&D using Sully’s as the live proving ground

Simple picture: Sully’s ≈ hosting under $100/mo + Helcim when cards are used + QuickBooks if you use it. Quiet Agency software + maintenance = $0 under this partnership.

4. Why this structure

  • Sully’s is a non-profit — Quiet Agency is contributing the platform as a donation of services
  • Your gym becomes the first live home for a boxing-first system (beyond generic gym SaaS)
  • You keep ownership of members, brand, and day-to-day ops; processors stay services
