# Sully's Digital Performance Platform — System Vision

**Status:** Blueprint v1.2  
**Public site (to redesign):** [https://www.sullysboxinggym.com](https://www.sullysboxinggym.com)  
**Framing:** Not “website + app.” One platform with a public face and an operating system behind it.

---

## The Opportunity

Sully's already owns what most gyms cannot buy: an **80+ year legacy** (EST 1943) — Canada's oldest boxing gym — and a lineage that includes training legends such as **Muhammad Ali, Lennox Lewis, George Chuvalo, Clyde Gray, Fern Bull, and Razor Ruddock**, while developing thousands of young athletes over decades.

The digital experience must:

1. **Tell that story** like a professional sports organization  
2. **Sell and operate** memberships, classes, kitchen, kids, and community  
3. **Run the building** as one live connected ecosystem  

Glofox-class tools do booking and billing. Sully's platform does **legacy + performance + facility OS**.

---

## One Stack, Many Surfaces

```
Website (public face)
        │
        ▼
Member Portal / Mobile App
        │
        ▼
Gym Operating System
        │
        ├── Front Desk + Command Center
        ├── Coach Tablets
        ├── Kitchen (KDS + orders)
        ├── Kids Program
        ├── Parent Portal
        ├── Digital Waivers
        ├── POS (adapter → deeper later)
        ├── Community Feed
        ├── Sully AI
        ├── Digital Signage / TV Network
        ├── TV Leaderboards
        ├── Boxing Passport / Legacy Wall / Trophy Room
        └── Analytics / Owner Office displays
```

**One database. One identity. One realtime bus.**  
Website, app, desk, TVs, kitchen, coaches, and owner dashboard all sync.

---

## Feel Like a Pro Sports Org

Everything — marketing site, member app, desk kiosk, TV network, kitchen ordering, leaderboards, coach tools — should feel like **one connected ecosystem**, not a neighborhood flyer site taped to a generic gym SaaS.

Visual system: [Brand Guidelines](../06-design/03-brand-guidelines.md) (heritage badge) + dark performance UI.

---

## Layer Definitions

| Layer | Job |
|-------|-----|
| **Website** | Recruit, inspire, educate, start join/trial; live schedule/events; legacy storytelling |
| **Member Portal / App** | Daily training hub (calendar), card, check-in, nutrition, progression, family, community |
| **Gym OS** | Staff operations: desk, coaches, kitchen, waivers, POS, signage, analytics |
